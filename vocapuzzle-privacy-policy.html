VocaPuzzle Privacy Policy

This privacy policy applies to the VocaPuzzle app (hereby referred to as "Application") for mobile devices that was created by Enes Çetin (hereby referred to as "Service Provider") as a Free service. This service is intended for use "AS IS".

Information Collection and Use

The Application is designed to operate locally on your device and respect user privacy. It does not collect, track, or transmit any personally identifiable information, IP addresses, or precise location data.

All game data—including level progress, unlocked realms, earned coins, saved vocabulary items, and user preferences—is stored exclusively on your device's local memory (Local Storage / AsyncStorage). No user data is transmitted to external cloud servers.

Third Party Access

The Application does not integrate third-party advertising networks, analytics tracking tools, or external software development kits (SDKs) that monitor or share user behavior with third parties.

Data Retention and Opt-Out Rights

Since all data is stored locally on your device, you can completely erase all stored game data at any time simply by clearing the application storage or uninstalling the Application via your device's standard app management settings.

Children's Privacy

The Application is an offline-safe educational word puzzle suitable for general audiences. The Service Provider does not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13 or any other age group.

Security

The Service Provider values your data protection. Because all game progress remains entirely on your local device, your information benefits directly from the native operating system security features built into your mobile device.
